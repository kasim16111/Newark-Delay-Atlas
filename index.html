Question 1 · Time of day

Delays snowball as the day goes on

A late inbound plane makes the next flight late, and the one after that. By the last departures of the night, nearly half of flights leave behind schedule.

Takeaway: book the first flight of the morning. A 6 a.m. departure was four times less likely to be late than a 10 p.m. one.

Question 2 · Season

Summer is when Newark breaks

Thunderstorms and peak travel volume pushed June and July far above every other month. Late fall was the calmest stretch of the year.

Question 3 · Airline

Which airline should you trust out of EWR?

United runs over half of Newark's flights, so its 27% late rate shapes the whole airport. Republic, which flies regional jets for United and American, was the most punctual but canceled most often. That tradeoff matters: a late flight still gets you there.

Question 4 · Route

The road to Silicon Valley is the bumpiest

Of Newark's 15 busiest routes, San Francisco had the highest late departure rate. Long-haul West Coast flights often leave in the evening rush, when delays are already stacking up.

How I built it

Data & method

The source is the 2023 NYC flights dataset, derived from U.S. Bureau of Transportation Statistics on-time records for every departure from EWR, JFK, and LGA. A flight counts as late when it departs 15 or more minutes after schedule, the federal standard. A flight with no departure time counts as canceled. Airlines with fewer than 1,000 Newark departures are excluded.
